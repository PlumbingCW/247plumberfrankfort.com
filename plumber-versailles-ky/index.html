Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

These four moves protect your Versailles home while the plumber is on the way. They take a few minutes and make a real difference to the damage.

Shut off the main water

Turn the main valve clockwise. It is usually near the meter or where the line enters the house. Open a low faucet to drain the pipes.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, switch off the breaker only if you can reach it dry. Otherwise stay out and call your utility.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the water's path. Lift furniture legs off wet carpet.

Call our 24/7 line

Tell dispatch what is leaking, where the shutoff is and whether anyone can meet the plumber at the door.

Water, sewer and what to ask your utility

Versailles homes are served by city utilities inside town, while rural properties may use private wells and septic systems. We do not assume which you have. If you are unsure who owns the line that is leaking, check your water bill or call the utility named on it, and ask where their responsibility ends and yours begins. A pipe between the meter and the house is generally the owner's.

If your water heater is working harder than it should, hardness could be part of the story. Ask your water provider for a current hardness figure or test your tap with a strip, and flush the tank on a schedule that fits the result.
