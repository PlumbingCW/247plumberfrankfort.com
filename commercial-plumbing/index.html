Emergency commercial plumbing in Frankfort, KY keeps shops, offices, restaurants and rental properties open. Backed-up floor drains, a burst supply line, a failed water heater and a dead restroom all cost money every hour they stay down. The 24/7 line connects you to dispatch at any hour, including holidays.

Commercial plumbing is different from residential work in scale, in code and in the people affected. Pipes are larger, fixtures are used more, and a failure can close a restroom or a kitchen to the public. We plan the repair around your business hours when we can and tell you plainly when the work will interrupt service.

Business owner: first steps in an emergency

  • Close the nearest isolating valve, or the main, to stop a leak. Show staff where it is.
  • Cordon off wet floors and post a sign, so no one slips.
  • Stop using affected restrooms or sinks. A full drain makes the problem worse.
  • If the problem touches a kitchen, check whether health rules require closing until it is fixed. Your local health department can say what applies.
  • Call dispatch with the business name, address, hours and whether staff can meet the plumber on site.

Downtown Frankfort has a long run of older commercial buildings, many dating from the nineteenth and early twentieth century, near the Capitol, along Main and St. Clair Streets and across the river in South Frankfort. Grease, backflow and other commercial responsibilities

Food service businesses must keep grease out of the sewer, and the local sewer provider's guidelines set the rules for traps and interceptors. Grease traps and interceptors collect it, and they need regular pumping. A trap that is neglected sends grease into the line and causes backups for the business and its neighbors. Ask your local sewer provider what rules apply to your trap and how often it must be serviced.

Backflow preventers protect the public water supply from contamination, and many utilities require periodic testing of them. Ask your water provider whether your device needs an annual test and who is certified to perform it.
