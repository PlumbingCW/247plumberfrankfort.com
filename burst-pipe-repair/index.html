Emergency burst pipe repair in Frankfort, KY begins with stopping the water. Find the main shutoff, turn it clockwise until it stops, and open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain the line. Then call our 24/7 dispatch number. We send a plumber as soon as the schedule allows, and you can ask about timing when you call.

Pipes burst for a handful of reasons: freezing water that expands inside the pipe, old galvanized or corroded lines that give way at a weak spot, high pressure, a nail or screw through a wall, and ground movement that stresses a buried line. Knowing which one applies tells us whether a single section needs to be cut out or whether the whole run is nearing the end of its life.

The first ten minutes after a pipe bursts

  • Shut the main water valve. It is usually where the line enters the house, near the meter, in a basement, crawl space or utility closet. Know where it is before an emergency, and try turning it once a year.
  • Open the lowest faucet and the highest faucet to bleed pressure and drain the line.
  • Turn off power to any wet room at the breaker panel only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water. If you cannot, call your electric utility or 911.
  • Turn off the water heater power or gas so the elements do not run dry.
  • Move furniture, electronics and papers out of the wet area. Mop or wet-vac standing water if it is safe to do so.
  • Photograph the damage before you clean up. Your insurance claim will need dated pictures.

Frozen pipes in a Kentucky winter

Frankfort's average January low is about 25 degrees Fahrenheit at the airport and closer to 22 downtown, and the record lows are well below zero. A hard freeze after a mild spell is the classic recipe for a burst pipe, because pipes in exterior walls, garages, crawl spaces and unheated basements are the first to freeze.

If a faucet runs slowly or not at all during a cold snap, a section may already be frozen. Open the tap, warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer or towels soaked in warm water, and never use a torch or open flame. If the pipe has already cracked, you will see water as soon as it thaws, so keep the main valve within reach.

To prevent a repeat, insulate exposed pipes, seal gaps where cold air enters near the plumbing, open cabinet doors under sinks on freezing nights and let a faucet drip when the temperature falls into the low teens. Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor hose bibs before the first freeze.

How we handle a burst pipe repair

  1. Control the water

    If you have not already shut it off, we find and close the right valve and isolate the damaged zone so the rest of the house can keep water.

  2. Find the failure and its extent

    We trace the pipe to see whether the split is a single point or part of a longer weak run, and check nearby joints for stress.

  3. Cut out and replace

    The damaged section comes out and new pipe goes in using fittings that match the material. We do not patch a split with tape or a clamp except as a short-term measure.

  4. Pressure test

    We restore water slowly and test the repair under pressure before we leave.

  5. Advise on cleanup

    We tell you what to dry and what to document. For large losses a water mitigation company handles drying and your insurer sets the claim process.

What the 1978 Kentucky River flood tells us about water damage

The Kentucky River crested at 48.47 feet in Frankfort in December 1978, the record for the city, and the water covered more than half of downtown. That flood came from outside the house. A burst pipe is a different kind of water, clean supply water that comes from inside the walls, and it follows a different insurance path.

Standard homeowner policies commonly cover sudden pipe bursts but not river flooding, which requires separate flood coverage. Read your policy or ask your agent which applies before you file.

What affects burst pipe repair cost

Pipe material

Copper, PEX, CPVC and galvanized all use different fittings and labor.

Access

A split behind finished drywall or under a slab needs openings, which are priced as their own step.

Length of the failed section

One split is simple. A run with several weak spots may be better replaced.

Time of day

Night, weekend and holiday dispatch carries an on-call charge, stated before we start.

Water damage cleanup

Plumbing repair and drying are separate jobs. We fix the pipe, and a mitigation company dries the structure.
