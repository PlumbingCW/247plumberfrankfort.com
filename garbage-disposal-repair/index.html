A disposal that hums, leaks or backs the sink up needs attention before food scraps start to rot. We repair and replace them.

Emergency garbage disposal repair in Frankfort, KY handles a unit that is jammed, humming, leaking or backing up into the other side of the sink. Turn the disposal off at the switch, and unplug it or switch off its breaker before you touch anything near the drain. Then call our 24/7 line.

Follow the manufacturer's guidelines for your model, since the reset button and the jam socket sit in different places on different units. Switching the unit off and unplugging it comes first every time.

A disposal is a simple machine with a motor, a flywheel, impellers and a drain connection. When it hums but does not turn, something has jammed the flywheel. When it leaks, a seal or the sink flange has failed. When the sink backs up, the clog may be in the disposal outlet or further down the line, and we trace it.

Disposal stuck or leaking: what to do first

  • Switch the disposal off at the wall and unplug it or turn off its breaker. Never put your hand in the opening.
  • Look under the sink for water. A drip from the bottom of the unit means a seal has failed and the unit may need replacing.
  • Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit once it has cooled. A tripped reset points to a jam or overheating.
  • Use a hex key in the center of the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth to free a jammed flywheel, if your model has that socket.
  • Do not use chemical drain cleaner. It damages seals and is hazardous if the unit must be opened.

What a disposal can and cannot handle

A disposal is designed for soft food scraps. Bones, fibrous vegetables such as celery and corn husks, coffee grounds in quantity, pasta and rice that swell, and grease or oil cause jams and clogs. Run cold water before, during and for several seconds after using the disposal so the waste moves into the main drain.

Fat poured into a disposal does not disappear. It cools in the drain and builds a plug. If your kitchen line keeps clogging, the cause is likely grease, and the drain cleaning page covers how we clear it.

Repair or replace a disposal

  1. Diagnose

    We check power, the reset, the flywheel and the connections to see what failed.

  2. Repair if it makes sense

    A jam, a loose connection or a worn gasket is repaired on the spot.

  3. Replace when needed

    A leaking body, a burned motor or a unit past its life is replaced with a new disposal that fits your mount.

  4. Test

    We run water, check for leaks at every joint and test the dishwasher drain connection.

Septic and sewer: does it matter for a disposal

Homes on septic systems need extra care with disposals, because more solids reach the tank and fill it faster. If your property is outside the city sewer, ask your septic pumper whether a disposal suits your system. Homes on the city sewer have fewer limits, though grease is a problem in either case.
