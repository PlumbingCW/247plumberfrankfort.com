Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Frankfort, KY

When every drain in the house is slow or the floor drain is bubbling, the main sewer line is the likely culprit. We clear it and check why it filled.

Call (855) 575-2890

Emergency sewer line cleaning in Frankfort, KY clears the main line that carries all of your household waste to the city sewer or septic tank. If every fixture is draining slowly, if a floor drain is bubbling, or if the lowest toilet gurgles when the washer runs, the main is likely partly blocked. Call our 24/7 line before it backs up completely.

Cleaning is a different job from repair. Cleaning removes what is in the pipe, such as grease, roots, wipes, scale and debris. Repair fixes the pipe itself.

We aim to clear the line first and then tell you, with a camera if needed, whether the line is healthy or whether it needs the work described on our sewer line repair page. A camera inspection is the usual way we make that call. We follow the equipment manufacturer's guidelines for cable and jetter use on each pipe type, since old clay and cast iron need gentler handling than new plastic.

While you wait for the plumber

  • Stop laundry, dishwasher and shower use. Flushing is a last resort.
  • Find the cleanout, a capped pipe near the foundation, in the basement or in the yard, and clear the area around it.
  • Do not pour chemical cleaners into a backed-up main. They do not reach the blockage and they are a hazard for the crew.
  • Keep a bucket and towels near the lowest drain to catch any overflow.
  • Tell us how old the house is and whether you know of earlier root problems.

Cable or jetter for a main line

A cable machine with a cutting head chews through roots and pushes a blockage free. It is fast and effective for a fresh clog. A jetter uses high-pressure water to wash the pipe wall clean, which is why it suits a main coated with grease and scale or a line that has clogged repeatedly. The hydro jetting page explains how that works.

On many calls we use both. The cable opens a channel so water can flow, and the jetter follows to clean the wall. Then we run water at volume to test the result before we put the machine away.

What goes down Frankfort drains

The most common main line blockages in homes anywhere are grease, wipes labeled flushable, paper towels, feminine products and roots. Wipes do not break up the way toilet paper does, and they catch on roots and rough joints and build into mats. Only toilet paper and human waste belong in a toilet.

Homes along the Kentucky River and in older neighborhoods add one more factor, pipe age. When the pipe is old, roots and rough walls catch debris faster. If your house is older, a camera look after cleaning is worth the time.

What a cleaning visit includes

  1. Find the access point

    We locate the cleanout or open a vent or fixture if none exists.

  2. Clear the blockage

    We cable or jet from the access point until flow returns.

  3. Test the flow

    We run water through several fixtures at once to confirm the line handles a normal load.

  4. Camera if it makes sense

    A camera pass shows the cause. It is recommended when roots, a sag or a repeat clog is suspected.

  5. Tell you what to expect

    We explain whether the line is healthy, needs scheduled cleaning, or needs repair.
