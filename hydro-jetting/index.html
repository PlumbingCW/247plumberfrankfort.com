Emergency Hydro Jetting in Frankfort, KY

When a cable keeps getting stuck or the drain returns to slow within weeks, jetting clears the pipe wall instead of poking a hole in the clog.

Call (855) 575-2890

Emergency hydro jetting in Frankfort, KY clears a backed-up main line or a heavily clogged drain using high-pressure water to scour the inside of the pipe. If sewage or gray water is coming up in a floor drain or tub, stop using water and call our 24/7 line. A plumber is sent as soon as the schedule allows.

Hydro jetting is not the first tool for every clog, and it is not a cure for a broken pipe. It is the right tool when buildup, grease or roots have narrowed the line and a cable only clears a small channel.

We look first, with a camera when the situation calls for it, and we jet only where it will help. We follow the equipment manufacturer's guidelines for nozzle choice and pressure, and we lower the pressure on old clay and cast iron.

Signs you need jetting, not another snaking

A cable cuts a hole through a blockage. If the pipe wall is coated with grease or mineral scale, the hole closes again and the drain clogs within weeks. People describe this as the plumber came, the drain worked, and then it was back. That is the signal to move up to jetting.

Other signs include a cable that stops short and cannot go further, gurgling from several fixtures at once, slow drains throughout the house, and floor drains that bubble when you run the washer. Roots entering through joints in older clay or cast iron can also be cut and flushed with a jetter, though roots will return if the joint stays open.

Backed up now: what to do while you wait

Stop adding water

Hold off on showers, laundry and the dishwasher. Every gallon goes into a line that has nowhere to carry it.

Protect the floor

Pull rugs and boxes away from the lowest drain, and put towels or a wet vac within reach.

Find the cleanout

A capped pipe in the basement, crawl space or yard is the cleanout. Knowing where it is saves time on arrival.

Tell us the history

Mention any earlier cable visits and how long each held. It helps us decide on jetting.

What a jetting visit involves

  1. Camera first, when it helps

    We run a camera to see the pipe condition. If the pipe is cracked or collapsed, jetting could make things worse, so we do not jet until we know.

  2. Cleanout and setup

    We connect the jetter hose at the cleanout and select a nozzle for the pipe size and the type of buildup.

  3. Jet in passes

    We work the nozzle forward and back, flushing debris toward the main. Roots, grease and scale are cut and carried out.

  4. Confirm with a second look

    After jetting we run water, and often a camera again, to confirm the pipe wall is clean.

Commercial lines and food service drains

Restaurant and institutional kitchens put more grease into a line than a household does. Jetting on a schedule keeps those lines open between grease trap services. If you run a shop or kitchen in downtown Frankfort, our commercial plumbing page explains the after-hours options.

Why older lines in Frankfort deserve a camera first

Neighborhoods such as South Frankfort and the historic districts near the Capitol have lines that may predate plastic pipe. Clay and cast iron age in ways that matter to jetting, because a weak section can break under pressure. A camera pass before jetting protects you from turning a clog into a collapse, and the recording helps us plan a repair if one is needed.
