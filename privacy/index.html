Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026.

Call (855) 575-2890

What this site collects

This website does not contain forms and does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect personal information through the pages themselves. Your browser sends standard technical information to our hosting provider when you load a page, such as your IP address and browser type, and the provider may keep server logs for security and performance.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows maps from Google Maps. Those services receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests them, and their own privacy policies apply to that information.

Phone calls

When you call our number, we may collect your phone number and the details you give us about the job, so that we can dispatch a plumber and follow up. Calls may be recorded or monitored for quality and training, and we tell you at the start of a call if recording is in use.

We use call information to provide the service you asked for, to improve our work and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and retention

We share information with service providers who help us run the business, such as phone and dispatch tools, and with the plumber assigned to your job. We share information when the law requires it. We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above.

Your choices and contact

You can ask us what information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, subject to legal requirements. Write to us at contact@247plumberfrankfort.com or call the number on this site. We may update this policy, and the date at the top shows the latest revision.
