Tankless Water Heater in Frankfort, KY

On-demand hot water works well when it is sized and maintained properly. We install, repair and descale tankless units.

Call (855) 575-2890

A tankless water heater in Frankfort, KY heats water as it flows instead of storing it in a tank. That removes standby heat loss and the risk of a tank flood, and it supplies hot water for as long as the unit can keep up. The trade-off is that tankless units depend on correct sizing, gas or electrical capacity, venting and regular descaling.

We install new units, repair existing ones, convert from a tank, and flush and descale heaters that have lost output. This page is organized around the questions callers ask us most, including when a flush is due, whether a unit can be repaired, and what installation involves.

Signs your tankless heater needs a flush or descale

The most common complaint is a heater that used to deliver steady hot water and now runs lukewarm, cycles on and off, or shows an error code. In many of those cases the heat exchanger has scale on it. Scale insulates the metal, reduces flow and makes the unit work harder than it should.

A flush circulates a descaling solution through the heat exchanger and then rinses it. It is routine maintenance, and it is the cheapest way to extend the life of the unit.

Manufacturer guidelines call for a flush on a schedule that depends on water hardness. Frankfort Plant Board's water quality report does not state a hardness figure, so ask the utility for its current number or test your tap and set your interval from the result.

Common tankless faults and where we look first

  • Error codes: we read the code, confirm what it means for your model and test the part it names.
  • Lukewarm or fluctuating water: scale, a clogged inlet filter, or a flow sensor that is not reading correctly.
  • Unit will not ignite on gas: gas supply, igniter or flame sensor, and venting.
  • Cold sandwich effect, where hot water arrives, cools, and then heats again: usually a flow or usage pattern issue we can adjust.
  • Noise or vibration: scale, a failing fan or loose venting.

Gas and electric tankless: what each demands from the house

A gas tankless unit burns a lot of fuel quickly, so the gas line serving it must be sized for the full burner input along with every other appliance. Many older homes have a line that was sized for a tank heater and a range. We calculate the load before we quote, because an undersized line is a leading cause of poor tankless performance.

An electric tankless unit needs a large dedicated circuit, often more than an existing panel can spare. In that case the panel work belongs in the scope of the project, and we say so up front rather than after the unit is on the wall.

Venting is the other limit. Condensing gas models vent through plastic pipe, and the run length and termination point must follow the manufacturer's rules. We lay out the vent path and tell you if the wall or the roof will be involved.

How tankless installation and conversion work

  1. Check the load and the lines

    We list what the unit must run at once and confirm the gas line or the circuit can supply it.

  2. Plan venting and condensate

    We choose the vent route and a drain for condensate, since condensing units produce acidic condensate that must go to a drain.

  3. Mount and connect

    The unit is mounted on the wall with service valves so a future flush does not require cutting pipe.

  4. Commission

    We test the burner or elements, check the temperature rise, confirm the flow rate, and show you the filter and the flush ports.

Freeze protection and tankless units

Kentucky winters bring hard freezes. In January the average daily low at Frankfort's Capital City Airport is about 25 degrees Fahrenheit, and past records have dropped well below zero. A tankless unit in an unheated garage or crawl space needs freeze protection, and a power outage during a cold snap can defeat built-in heaters. Keep the unit in a conditioned space where possible, and see our burst pipe repair page for what to do if a line freezes.

If you are weighing a conversion from a tank, our water heater installation page explains the tank side, and our crews in Lawrenceburg and Versailles see the same freeze conditions as Frankfort. A unit that fails in a freeze is a job for water heater repair.
