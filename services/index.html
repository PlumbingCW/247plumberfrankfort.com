How to choose between emergency and planned work

Emergency work is anything where water is spreading, sewage is backing up, a gas appliance is not safe or a business cannot operate. Planned work is anything you can schedule, such as replacing an aging water heater before it fails or adding a fixture during a remodel. Planned work is priced in writing before it starts, and emergency work is priced once we see the problem.

If a planned job turns out to have an emergency cause, we say so. A slow drain that is really a failing sewer line moves from a cleaning call to a sewer line repair estimate, and a water heater that leaks from the tank moves from repair to replacement.
