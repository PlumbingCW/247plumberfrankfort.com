Emergency Faucet Repair in Frankfort, KY

A faucet that will not shut off or sprays under the sink is a leak you can stop with a valve. Then call us.

Call (855) 575-2890

Emergency faucet repair in Frankfort, KY starts when a faucet will not shut off, a handle breaks, or water sprays from under the sink. Close the small valves under the sink, turning them clockwise, and the faucet loses pressure. If the valves do not close, shut the main. Then call our 24/7 line.

Most faucet repairs are small. A drip comes from a worn cartridge, washer, seat or O-ring. A loose handle usually needs a set screw or a new cartridge.

A spray under the sink points to a supply line or compression nut. We match the repair to the faucet and replace the faucet itself when parts are no longer available or the finish has failed.

Faucet leaking badly: quick steps

  • Close the two valves under the sink, hot and cold, turning clockwise. Open the faucet to confirm the water stopped.
  • If the valves leak or will not close, shut the main water valve.
  • Put a bucket under any drip and wipe cabinets dry. Standing moisture can swell a cabinet base.
  • Photograph the damage if water reached floors or cabinets.
  • Call and tell us the brand if you know it, and whether the faucet is a single handle or two handle.

Dripping faucets cost more than they seem

A faucet that drips once a second can waste thousands of gallons in a year, and the water is treated, pumped and billed. Frankfort Plant Board bills by usage, so a drip appears on every bill. According to the EPA's WaterSense program, a labeled bathroom faucet flows at 1.5 gallons per minute or less. Fixing a drip is one of the cheapest ways to recover that cost.

Mineral scale accelerates wear on cartridges and seats. If your hot water faucet drips more than the cold, scale from the water heater may be involved, and a flush of the tank is worth doing. See water heater repair if the heater is old.

How we repair a faucet

  1. Identify the faucet

    We find the brand and model so we use the correct parts.

  2. Isolate and open

    We shut the local valves and take the faucet apart.

  3. Replace worn parts

    We replace cartridges, seats, O-rings and supply lines as needed.

  4. Test

    We run hot and cold, check the handle through its full range and look for any drips below.

  5. Recommend replacement only when needed

    If the faucet is corroded or parts are no longer made, we tell you why replacement is the better choice.

Hose bibs and winter in Frankfort

An outdoor hose bib that is not freeze-protected can split in the first hard freeze, even when you cannot see it. The water stays frozen in the pipe until a thaw, and then it leaks inside the wall. Disconnect the hose before winter, shut the inside valve if one exists and leave the outdoor valve open to drain.

If a hose bib leaks inside a wall after a cold snap, shut the main and call us. See also burst pipe repair for what to expect.
