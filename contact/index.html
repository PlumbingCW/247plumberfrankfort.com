24/7 Emergency Line Open

Contact Frankfort 24/7 Plumbing

For anything urgent, call. The line is answered every hour of every day.

Call (855) 575-2890

The fastest way to reach us is by phone. Dispatch answers around the clock, and a plumber is sent as soon as the schedule allows. We take requests by phone so that we can ask what is happening and tell you how to protect your home right away.

For non-urgent questions, such as a quote for planned work or a billing question, you can email us and we reply during business hours. If water is spreading or sewage is backing up, do not wait for an email. Call.

What to have ready when you call

  • The address of the property and the best phone number to reach you.
  • What is happening: a leak, a backup, no hot water or something else.
  • Where the main water shutoff is, and whether you have closed it.
  • Whether anyone can meet the plumber at the door.
  • The make and fuel of the water heater if the call is about hot water.

Where to find us

We have no walk-in office, so please reach us by phone or email. The phone line is the fastest way to ask about timing.

See our service areas page for the towns we cover, or our services page for the work we do. Both pages are written for homeowners and list what to expect on a visit.
