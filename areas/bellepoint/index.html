Bellepoint is a small neighborhood on the west side of the Kentucky River in Frankfort. Public sources give its general location but not exact boundaries, so we describe it by its landmarks and do not draw a line.

If you live or own property in the area and have a plumbing problem, this page covers what is documented. It also explains how we can help from Frankfort.

Where Bellepoint is

Wikipedia describes Bellepoint as a small neighborhood on the west bank, north of Benson Creek and across the river from downtown Frankfort. Franklin County's 2016 plan lists a planning overlay for "Bellpoint and Benson Valley," which spells the name differently.

A former Frankfort Independent school was located in the neighborhood. The spelling and exact boundaries vary by source, so use your street address and not the neighborhood name when you call.

Why water questions come up

The City of Frankfort's flood protection guide names Bellepoint among the areas it discusses for flooding. It sits on the west bank near Benson Creek, and the guide is concerned with creek and river water.

That is a documented citywide flood reference and not a claim that your home has water problems. It does mean that sump pump and drainage questions are worth asking before the wet season, not after it.

Practical guidance for a basement that takes on water

Start by finding out whether the water comes from rain, from the river or from a plumbing leak, because each has a different fix. A pump and drain system helps with groundwater and rain, while a leak needs a pipe repair. Our sump pump service begins with a test of the pit, the pump and the discharge line.

The city's guide notes that a plug or standpipe can handle one to two feet of sewer backup. Deeper backup calls for an overhead sewer or a backup valve. Check that your homeowner policy does not exclude flood damage, because many do.
