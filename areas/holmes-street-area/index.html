Holmes Street is a named corridor in Frankfort that appears in the county's planning documents and in the city's flood guide. Homes and businesses share the street, so the plumbing calls here range from household drains to small commercial repairs.

This page is deliberately modest about boundaries, because we could only verify the street and its general context, not a defined neighborhood line. We serve it from Frankfort.

What is documented about the area

Franklin County's 2016 plan lists Holmes Street as a priority corridor in a planning overlay. The City of Frankfort's flood protection guide also names the Holmes Street area among the places it discusses.

A former school stood on Holmes Street, and the addresses along it include both residences and businesses. Beyond that, we have not verified boundaries, housing ages or construction types.

Homes and businesses on the same street

A mixed corridor means a plumber may be asked to handle a bathroom fixture in the morning and a shop or office drain in the afternoon. The skills overlap, but the details differ. Commercial properties often have larger drain lines, more fixtures and different access rules.

Our commercial plumbing service covers small businesses. The residential services below cover the homes nearby.

Practical guidance before you call

Note where the problem shows up and how many fixtures are affected. One slow drain usually points to a local clog, while several backed-up fixtures suggest a blockage further down the line. Tell us whether the property is a home, a rental or a business, because it changes who needs to approve the work.

For rental or commercial spaces, check your lease for who handles plumbing repairs and who must be notified. Take a photo of any leak or damage before the plumber arrives.
