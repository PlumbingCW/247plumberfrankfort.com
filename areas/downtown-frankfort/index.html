Downtown Frankfort is the state capital's business and government core, and it includes several nationally recognized historic districts. Plumbing here is mostly about shops, offices, restaurants and upper-floor apartments, with some homes at the edges.

We serve downtown from Frankfort. We describe the work and the price before it starts, and you decide whether to go ahead.

What downtown Frankfort includes

The National Register lists the Central Frankfort Historic District, added in 2009 and running along East and West Second, Logan, High and Mero streets to the river. The Old Statehouse Historic District was listed in 1980, and the Frankfort Commercial Historic District in 1979 spans both sides of the river at Bridge Street.

A Corner in Celebrities Historic District was listed in 1971. The county county plan describes downtown as including Capitol Avenue and St. Clair Street.

Commercial and mixed-use plumbing

Older commercial buildings often have several tenants, shared drain lines and limited access. A problem in one unit can show up in another, so the first job is to find where the line is shared. We start with a camera or a cable and report what we find.

Our commercial plumbing service covers repairs, fixtures and drain work for small businesses. Restaurants and shops that fight repeat clogs may benefit from hydro jetting, which clears buildup that a cable leaves behind.

Combined sewers and heavy rain

Frankfort has a combined sewer system, and the Kentucky Energy and Environment Cabinet's list shows 14 permitted combined sewer overflow outfalls under permit KY0022861, as of January 2019. In a combined system, a heavy storm can load the pipes that serve low floors and basements.

If a lower-level drain backs up during a storm, call us and your property manager. Keep valuables off the floor and avoid contact with the water, which can carry waste.
